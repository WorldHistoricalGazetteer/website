WHG converts place names to IPA so that it can match them across writing systems. That conversion is driven by grapheme→IPA rule sets — one per language and script — and their quality varies enormously. Where the rules fail, matching on those scripts performs no better than chance, while methods that know no phonetics at all still work. It is a gap we can close, and closing it needs people who read the languages.
