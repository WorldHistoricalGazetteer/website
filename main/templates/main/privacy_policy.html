Privacy

You may use WHG freely without registering. If you choose to register, we require a name and a valid email address, which we will never share without your explicit permission. We may occasionally notify registered users of changes to the system or impending downtime that could affect their use of it.

Use of Turnstile

To protect against automated abuse (bots), WHG uses Cloudflare Turnstile. When Turnstile is displayed (for example, on forms submitted by unregistered users), your browser may communicate with Cloudflare’s servers to verify that you are a human visitor. This may involve the transmission of limited technical information (such as IP address and browser characteristics). Cloudflare does not use this information for advertising or cross-site tracking; it is used solely to ensure the security and integrity of WHG services.

ORCID Registration and Sign-In

If you choose to register or sign in using your ORCID iD, WHG will — with your permission — access your public ORCID record (name, iD, and affiliation) and any information visible to “Trusted parties”, including at least one verified email address. This information is stored securely in your WHG profile and used only for account management and necessary communication. After your account is created, we will ask separately for your consent to receive optional newsletters. You may revoke WHG’s access at any time through your ORCID account settings.

Invitations sent by our users

Signed-in WHG users can email someone a link to a WHG page, or an invitation to register. If you have received such an email, it was sent at the request of an individual user, whose name appears in it.

We do not store the email address it was sent to. The address is used to send that one message and is then discarded — it is not saved in our database, not written to our logs, and not copied into any of our internal systems. The sender’s own email address is never disclosed to you, and yours is never disclosed to the sender.

So that we can honour a request not to be contacted again — and stop anyone being invited repeatedly — we keep a one-way scrambled form (a salted cryptographic hash) of the address, from which the address itself cannot practicably be recovered. A record that an invitation was sent, identified only by that scrambled form, is kept for 90 days and then deleted; an opt-out is kept indefinitely, because forgetting it would mean contacting you again. Every invitation contains a link to opt out, and users are limited to five invitations per day.

Usage Analytics

WHG uses a self-hosted, privacy-first analytics tool (Plausible) to understand overall site usage and improve our services. It is cookieless, does not track you across websites, and does not collect or store any personally identifying information; all measurements are aggregate. In interactive tools such as Map your Data we additionally record a small number of anonymous interaction events — for example, that a dataset was imported or that reconciliation was run — so we can see where the workflow can be improved. These events never include the contents of your data: in Map your Data your data stays in your own browser and is sent to WHG only if you explicitly choose to contribute it.
