Reconciliation beta

Take a tabular place dataset from raw file to reconciled, submission-ready gazetteer — entirely in your browser. Works for a single place or thousands of rows.

Your dataset stays on this device, in this browser. Nothing is uploaded. When reconciliation is wired up, only minimal, de-duplicated queries will be sent to WHG — never your rows — and only the rows you explicitly choose will ever be contributed.
