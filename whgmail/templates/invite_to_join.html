{{ inviter_name }} has invited you to join the World Historical Gazetteer (WHG).

WHG links, visualises and shares data about places throughout history. It helps researchers, teachers and students connect records of the same place across different sources, languages and periods — and publish their own historical place data so that others can find and build on it.

Create your free WHG account

That link goes to our Login page, which doubles as the sign-up page — you don't need an account already.

Signing up with ORCiD. WHG accounts are created using an ORCiD iD — a free, persistent identifier widely used by researchers, which lets us keep the platform free of automated sign-ups. If you don't have one, registering takes a couple of minutes and is useful well beyond WHG. You do not need to be an academic to get one. Our sign-up page explains exactly what WHG reads from your ORCiD record, and answers the common questions.

Meanwhile, you are very welcome to explore without an account:

  • The WHG home page — what the project is and who it's for
  • Explore places — search and browse the historical gazetteers we index
  • Documentation — guides, tutorials and technical detail
