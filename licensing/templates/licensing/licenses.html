Licences in the World Historical Gazetteer

Every dataset, collection and reference gazetteer in WHG carries an explicit licence describing how its data may be reused. This page lists all {{ license_count }} licences currently recognised by the system, so that contributors can choose the terms under which they share their work, and so that anyone reusing WHG data can see exactly what each licence permits. Each entry links to the official licence text, including non-English versions where the licence publishes them.

🤝 “By arrangement” — beyond the licence

When you contribute data through the WHG Workbench, you can add two optional conditions on top of your chosen licence: allow commercial use and/or adaptations by arrangement. This signals that although the licence itself doesn't grant these, you'll consider requests case-by-case — WHG passes enquiries to you and your contact details stay private. Look for the note on the licences below where this applies.

Choosing a licence for your own contribution? More open terms (public domain or Attribution) let the widest range of scholars, students and tools reuse your work; ShareAlike keeps derivatives open; NonCommercial and NoDerivatives limit reuse. If you are unsure, WHG recommends an Attribution licence such as CC BY 4.0. Ready to share data? See Contribute to WHG. This list is generated directly from WHG's licence vocabulary and updates as it grows.
