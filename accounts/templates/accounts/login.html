Sign in with ORCiD

New here? Signing in creates your account. It's free, takes a couple of minutes, and anyone can — you don't need to be an academic.

WHG uses ORCiD (Open Researcher and Contributor ID) for sign-in, so your contributions stay linked to a verified scholarly identity.

You do not need an account to search WHG's indexes or to view datasets and collections. An account is required only to contribute data, create collections, or use our APIs. With your consent, it also lets us tell you about important updates.

Not at all. Anyone can obtain an ORCiD iD, free of charge, regardless of affiliation or professional status — and use it to contribute to WHG. Signing up takes just a few minutes and needs only minimal information.

No problem — just Sign in with ORCiD above. During sign-in we'll offer to link your existing WHG account, along with all its datasets and collections, to your ORCiD iD. You only need to do this once, and nothing is lost.

The first time you sign in with ORCiD, we ask your permission to:

  • Access your public ORCiD record (name, ORCiD iD, and affiliation) — already visible on your ORCiD profile.
  • Read any other information you've set to “Trusted parties” visibility.
  • Store this securely-encrypted in your WHG profile.
  • Separately, and only afterwards, we'll ask your consent to receive optional newsletters.

ORCiD provides a persistent, unique identifier that links your contributions to your scholarly profile, ensuring proper attribution and protecting the provenance of the data you add. It also strengthens security and reduces fraudulent or automated sign-ups, protecting our shared computing resources and the integrity of the WHG community.
